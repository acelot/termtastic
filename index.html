termtastic

highlights

  • Vibecode-free

    Fully handwritten code. Every line was typed by a human.

  • Single binary

    Dependency-free application. Download, run, done.

  • Fully asynchronous

    Built on Tokio channels. The UI never waits for the radio.

  • Small screens

    Optimized for tiny terminals, down to 80×24 characters.

  • Your colors

    Respects the terminal palette. No opinionated TrueColor schemes.

  • Keyboard-centric

    Navigate every screen without touching the mouse.

  • Plain ASCII UI

    Minimum use of emoji and non-ASCII characters in the interface.

  • Small footprint

    Tiny memory usage, around 20 KB.

  • Multiple protocols

    Connect to your node over TCP, BLE or Serial.

  • Auto discovery

    Finds paired BLE devices, TCP devices via mDNS and Serial ports.

  • System directories

    Config, database and logs live where your OS expects them.
